2. Summary (plain language)

  • Guide Sight helps people with low vision by describing images, reading text aloud, and helping manage day-to-day tasks and medications.
  • To describe what your camera sees or transcribe your voice, we send that image or audio to trusted AI providers for processing. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.
  • Most of your personal content (medications, health readings, notes, contacts) is stored locally on your device, not on our servers.
  • You can ask us to access or delete your data at any time.
  • Guide Sight is not a medical device — never rely on it for safety-critical or medical decisions.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Camera images / video frames — captured when you use Live Assist. Used to generate descriptions.
  • Voice recordings — captured when you ask a question by voice. Used to transcribe your speech.
  • Health and medication information — medications, dosages, schedules, expiry dates, and health readings you enter. This may constitute special-category / sensitive data.
  • Notes and contacts — notes you save and emergency/responder contacts you add.
  • Account information — if you sign in, your name, email address, and authentication identifier.
  • Preferences — settings such as theme, contrast, voice, and detail level.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Purchase/subscription data — managed by RevenueCat and the app store. We receive subscription status and transaction identifiers, not your payment card details.
  • Device and diagnostic data — app version, device model, OS, crash logs used to operate and improve the App.

3.3 Where your content is stored

  • On your device: medications, health readings, notes, contacts, and preferences are stored locally using the device's local storage.
  • In transit for processing only: images and audio are transmitted to our backend and onward to AI providers solely to produce a result, then returned to your device.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the App's core features (image description, text reading, voice transcription, medication and task management);
  • Transcribe your voice and analyse images to answer your questions;
  • Remember your preferences and content;
  • Process subscriptions and prevent abuse;
  • Diagnose problems, maintain security, and improve the App;
  • Respond to your requests and provide support;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your camera images, voice, health data, or notes for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

9. Your rights
